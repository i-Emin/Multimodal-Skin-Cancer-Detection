## Critical synthesis

The central evidence supports testing metadata as a conditional contribution, not assuming it always helps. Ahammed's Table 5 shows a gain for transfer EfficientNet-B3 but losses for some other transfer backbones. Yap et al. show a melanoma AUC decrease when metadata is added to their two-image model. These outcomes motivate matched modality controls rather than comparing a multimodal score with an unrelated single-image baseline. Das et al. broaden the design space to attention and gating, but their described random split and internally inconsistent split diagram make protocol auditing essential before attributing a gain to fusion alone. Our project therefore begins with a transparent concatenation baseline and tests robustness to unavailable metadata; the split overlap limits any generalization claim.
