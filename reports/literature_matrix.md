# Literature review: source-verified starting matrix

Research question: Does clinical metadata improve skin lesion classification, and under which fusion and evaluation conditions? This is a **reading matrix**, not a completed critical review. We will fill protocol, objective, resource, and limitation details after close reading of each full paper. Scores across these papers are not directly comparable because datasets, modalities, labels, and splits differ.

| Paper | Question and contribution | Modalities and assumptions | Mechanism and objective | Data and protocol | Baselines and evidence | Resources and reproducibility | Limitations and connections |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Ahammed et al., 2025](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/MULA2025/html/Ahammed_Skin_Lesion_Classification_Using_Dermoscopic_Images_and_Clinical_Metadata_Insights_CVPRW_2025_paper.html) | Curates ISIC-DICM-17K and tests metadata's contribution; **reproduction target** | Dermoscopic images; age, sex, anatomical site | Image-only versus late-fusion image+metadata across multiple backbones; full loss details to extract | ISIC-DICM-17K, 17,060 images; 15,098 train, 1,962 validation; ISIC 2020 test via Kaggle | Supervised and transfer-learning models; extract exact comparable rows before choosing target | [Dataset and source repository](https://github.com/mmu-dermatology-research/isic-dicm-17k); verify split IDs and code availability | Original test labels are not publicly available in the paper's setup; investigate patient/lesion overlap and split recoverability |
